## Outcome and audience

11 September addendum: [Brand-first AI designer](brand-designer.md) defines the approved positioning, material/reference inputs, selective preference memory and next delivery priority. It supersedes language-first benchmark sequencing; separate stage approvals remain mandatory.

Kely helps an owner-led small business turn an idea, business update or customer question into a credible, well-designed social post without hiring a designer or learning an editor. Target businesses include salons, cafes, cleaning services, startups and SaaS products. Do not promise reach, sales, replacement of a marketing team or verified market demand.

The first delivery is an application running locally on Robert's Mac with a complete testable journey, real AI capability behind explicit cost controls, and simulated/test billing. Robert accepts the local product before any production work begins.

## First release

- One owner account and one business; no teams or multiple brands.
- English interface. Conversation and generated copy follow the user's message language, with an explicit language request taking precedence over automatic detection. A website's language never overrides the user's choice.
- LinkedIn and Instagram static posts: one image or a carousel of at most six slides. Six is Kely's initial product cap, not a platform limit.
- Idea-first onboarding. Optional business URL or free-text context. An alternate entry helps people who do not have a post idea.
- Adaptive, concise questions; save useful business facts, voice and visual preferences for the next post.
- Approve content before visual work. Approve a first-slide style preview before generating the remaining slides. Reuse the approved cover.
- All revisions requested in chat. No canvas, drag-and-drop, layer inspector or manual copy editor in this release.
- Images, real screenshots, illustrations or typography-only layouts selected per post; do not impose screenshots on every business.
- Save draft, conversation, approved outline, design versions, artifacts and history. Revisions preserve the last successful version.
- Export caption text, PNG images, a downloadable image bundle and a PDF for LinkedIn carousel sharing. Downloads of existing artifacts do not trigger AI charges.
- One target platform per result; adapting an existing post to another platform is a separately quoted action with existing context reused.
- A repeat-post entry reuses saved facts/style and relevant previous topics. Suggestions are part of a quoted action or previously stored, not an invisible background expense.

## Main journey

1. Landing page demonstrates curated examples and invites a post idea. Preserve the idea locally before signup/payment.
2. Account creation and plan selection. Explain weekly credits and action costs before purchase. Real personalized AI, URL fetching and business analysis do not run before entitlement.
3. Short preparation conversation collects only the missing information needed for a useful post. A bounded preparation action is quoted before the first paid call, including when the person abandons the conversation.
4. Present the post angle, caption and slide copy, separating sourced facts from suggestions. Request factual/content approval.
5. Quote and produce a cover/style preview. Allow chat feedback with any additional charge visible before starting.
6. After cover approval, quote the remaining visuals if not already included in an accepted stage quote. Never silently run unapproved later stages.
7. Show result, exports, version history and a clear next-post action. Low balance offers upgrade first, a one-off pack second.

Local founder testing must not require a real subscription purchase. Fixture entitlements must be clearly labeled; eventual Stripe Sandbox payment must still exercise server-confirmed access, not trust a success URL.

## Explicit exclusions

No Stories, video, advertising creative workflow, automatic publishing, social account integrations, scheduling, social analytics, mandatory content calendar, autonomous website monitoring, email engagement automation, graphic editor, teams, public deployment or production support automation.

Domain kely.io is a candidate, not owned or legally cleared. Seller entity, tax/legal obligations, live payment provider terms and production privacy policies are separate pre-launch decisions. Internal sample testing does not authorize public use of a client's brand, testimonials or assets.

## Product acceptance

- A new account can reach an exported post without leaving the app to repair copy/design.
- The app explains which stage runs, what it costs, what is preserved and what to do after failure or insufficient credits.
- User approvals bind to the exact current content/style version. Editing approved content invalidates affected later approvals.
- Two accounts cannot see, modify or download one another's business data, publications or assets.
- Reload/restart restores saved state and never automatically repeats an uncertain paid request.
- Desktop is the primary workspace; mobile supports the same journey without horizontal overflow or unreachable actions.
- Quality is assessed with actual generations, not only curated screenshots. Unknown or failed quality checks stay visibly unaccepted.
